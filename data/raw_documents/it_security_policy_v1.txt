1. PURPOSE
================================================================

This policy establishes the minimum security standards
required to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and
availability of Northstar Analytics Group ("Northstar" or "the
Company") information systems, data assets, and supporting
infrastructure. It defines mandatory technical and behavioral
controls applicable to all personnel and supplements the
specific procedures defined in the Device Usage Policy
(IT-POL-0211), Access Control Policy (IT-POL-0224), and
Incident Reporting Standard Operating Procedure
(SOC-SOP-0418).

================================================================
2. SCOPE
================================================================

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, interns,
and third parties who access Northstar information systems,
process Company data, or operate equipment connected to
Company networks, regardless of work location. It governs all
Company-owned devices, approved personal devices, cloud
environments, and on-premises infrastructure. Local statutory
or regulatory requirements supersede this policy where they
impose stricter controls.

================================================================
3. PASSWORD RULES
================================================================

All passwords used to access Company systems must comply with
the following standards:

   - Minimum length of 14 characters
   - At least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one
     number, and one special character
   - Must not contain the user's name, username, or any
     dictionary word longer than 5 characters
   - Must not reuse any of the previous 12 passwords
   - Maximum lifetime of 365 days for standard accounts and 90
     days for privileged accounts
   - Must be stored only in the Company-approved password
     manager; storage in browsers, text files, or paper notes
     is prohibited

Shared accounts are prohibited except in documented service
account scenarios approved by the Access Control governance
process (IT-POL-0224, Section 6).

================================================================
4. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA) REQUIREMENTS
================================================================

Multi-factor authentication is required for all Company
systems, including but not limited to corporate email,
single sign-on, VPN, source code repositories, cloud
infrastructure consoles, and any system processing customer
data. Approved authentication factors are: Company-issued
hardware security keys, the Company-approved mobile
authenticator application, and platform-bound passkeys on
managed devices. SMS-based one-time codes are prohibited
except as a documented temporary fallback authorized by the
Security Operations Center. Privileged accounts must use a
hardware security key as the primary factor. Loss of an MFA
device must be reported immediately to the IT Service Desk so
the factor can be revoked.

================================================================
5. APPROVED SOFTWARE RULES
================================================================

Employees may not install unapproved software on Company
devices. The current list of approved applications is
maintained in the Company Software Catalog and is updated
monthly by IT Architecture. Requests for new software must be
submitted through the Software Request Portal and require
review for license compliance, security posture, and data
handling implications. Browser extensions are treated as
software and follow the same approval process. Software
identified as unapproved on a Company device will be removed
automatically by endpoint management tooling, and repeated
violations may result in disciplinary action.

================================================================
6. APPROVED VENDOR RULES
================================================================

Devices and peripherals that connect to Company systems,
process Company data, or transmit data over Company networks
must be purchased from a vendor listed in the current IT
Vendor Catalog (Appendix B) or be individually approved in
writing by IT Procurement. This requirement applies regardless
of funding source, including expense reimbursement under the
Expense and Reimbursement Policy (FIN-POL-0117, Section 6).
Cloud and SaaS vendors are subject to a separate vendor risk
assessment process; no Company data may be transmitted to a
SaaS vendor that has not completed the assessment and been
added to the approved SaaS register.

================================================================
7. DEVICE SECURITY
================================================================

All devices used to access Company systems must meet the
following baseline controls:

   - Full-disk encryption enabled
   - Company-managed endpoint detection and response (EDR)
     agent installed and active
   - Operating system and applications patched within 14 days
     of vendor release for standard patches and 72 hours for
     critical patches
   - Screen lock enabled with a maximum 5-minute inactivity
     timeout
   - Local administrator privileges restricted to IT-managed
     accounts

Personal devices, including phones and tablets, require formal
security approval before they may access Company systems.
Approved personal devices must be enrolled in the Company
mobile device management (MDM) platform and are subject to
remote wipe in the event of loss, theft, or separation.
Detailed device handling requirements are defined in the
Device Usage Policy (IT-POL-0211).

================================================================
8. NETWORK SECURITY
================================================================

Connections to Company systems from outside a Company facility
must be established through the Company VPN or an approved
zero-trust access broker. Public, untrusted, and hotel
networks may only be used in combination with VPN; direct
connection to Company resources over such networks is
prohibited. Personal hotspots are acceptable when other secure
options are unavailable. Employees may not configure
unauthorized network equipment, including consumer-grade
routers or unmanaged switches, to bridge Company devices to
external networks. Any suspected network compromise must be
treated as a security incident and reported in accordance with
Section 10.

================================================================
9. PHISHING REPORTING
================================================================

All employees are required to report suspected phishing
emails using the "Report Phish" button available in the
Company email client. Suspected phishing must be reported
within 24 hours of receipt or identification. Employees should
not forward suspected phishing emails to colleagues, click any
links, or download any attachments, including for the purpose
of inspection. Confirmed phishing incidents are tracked by the
Security Operations Center and contribute to ongoing user
awareness training assignments.

================================================================
10. SECURITY INCIDENT REPORTING
================================================================

A security incident is any event that may compromise the
confidentiality, integrity, or availability of Company
information or systems. Examples include lost or stolen
devices, suspected account compromise, unauthorized data
disclosure, malware infection, and unauthorized physical
access to restricted areas. Lost or stolen devices must be
reported immediately and no later than 1 hour from discovery.
All other security incidents must be reported within 1 hour
of discovery to the Security Operations Center via the
Incident Reporting hotline or portal. Detailed triage,
containment, and post-incident review steps are defined in
the Incident Reporting SOP (SOC-SOP-0418). Incidents involving
personal data are additionally governed by the Data Privacy
Policy (DPO-POL-0309) and may trigger regulatory notification
obligations.

================================================================
11. EXCEPTIONS
================================================================

Exceptions to this policy may be granted only where a
documented business need exists and compensating controls are
in place. All exceptions must be requested through the
Security Exception Management process and approved by the
CISO or a delegated authority. Exceptions are time-bound, do
not exceed 12 months without re-review, and do not establish
precedent.
