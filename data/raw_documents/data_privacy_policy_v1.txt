1. PURPOSE
================================================================

This policy establishes the standards governing the collection,
classification, storage, use, sharing, and disposal of personal
and confidential information at Northstar Analytics Group
("Northstar" or "the Company"). It is intended to protect the
privacy rights of customers, employees, and other data
subjects, to support compliance with applicable data protection
laws including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and equivalent regional
frameworks, and to define behavioral expectations that
complement the technical controls described in the IT Security
Policy (IT-POL-0203).

================================================================
2. SCOPE
================================================================

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, interns,
and third parties who access, process, or otherwise handle
personal or confidential information belonging to Northstar,
its customers, its employees, or its business partners,
regardless of work location or device. It governs data in all
forms, including electronic, printed, verbal, and visual.
Local statutory or regulatory requirements supersede this
policy where they impose stricter obligations.

================================================================
3. CONFIDENTIAL DATA CLASSIFICATION
================================================================

All information handled at Northstar is classified into one of
four tiers, with handling requirements escalating accordingly:

   - Public:        Approved for unrestricted external release
                    (marketing materials, published research)
   - Internal:      Default classification for business
                    communications; not for external release
                    without approval
   - Confidential:  Customer data, employee personal data,
                    financial records, internal strategy,
                    source code, and unreleased analytics
                    outputs
   - Restricted:    Regulated data including payment card
                    information, government identifiers,
                    health information, and authentication
                    secrets

Employees are responsible for applying the correct
classification at the point of creation and for handling
received data according to its existing classification. When
in doubt, data must be treated as Confidential pending
clarification from the Data Protection Office.

================================================================
4. CUSTOMER DATA HANDLING RULES
================================================================

Customer data is classified as Confidential at minimum and may
be Restricted depending on its contents. The following rules
apply to all customer data:

   - Customer data must not be stored on personal devices
     under any circumstances, including personal laptops,
     phones, tablets, or removable media
   - Customer reports, including analytics outputs,
     dashboards, and exports, must be stored only in approved
     Company systems as listed in the Approved Systems
     Register
   - Customer data must not be transmitted through personal
     email accounts, consumer messaging applications, or
     unapproved file-sharing services
   - Access to customer data must follow least-privilege
     principles; access is granted on the basis of
     demonstrated business need and is reviewed quarterly by
     data owners
   - Customer data may not be used for purposes outside the
     scope of the contractual engagement without documented
     customer consent

================================================================
5. EMPLOYEE DATA HANDLING RULES
================================================================

Employee personal data is processed for human resources,
payroll, benefits, performance management, and statutory
compliance purposes. Such data is treated as Confidential and,
where it includes regulated categories such as health
information or government identifiers, as Restricted.
Employees have the right to access their own personal data and
to request correction of inaccurate information through the
People Operations Service Desk. Managers and HR personnel with
access to employee data may use it only for documented
business purposes and must not discuss it outside authorized
channels.

================================================================
6. DATA RETENTION RULES
================================================================

All Company data is subject to defined retention periods
maintained in the Records Retention Schedule. Standard
retention periods include:

   - Customer engagement records:  7 years post-engagement
   - Financial transaction records: 7 years
   - Employee records:              7 years post-separation
   - System and access logs:        13 months minimum
   - Marketing and prospect data:   2 years from last contact
   - Personal data subject requests: 3 years from resolution

Retention periods may be extended where legal holds are
issued by the General Counsel's office. Data must be securely
destroyed at the end of its retention period using approved
deletion methods; informal retention "just in case" is
prohibited.

================================================================
7. DATA SHARING RULES
================================================================

Internal sharing of Confidential and Restricted data must
follow least-privilege principles and use only approved
collaboration tools as defined in the IT Security Policy
(IT-POL-0203, Section 5). External sharing of Confidential or
Restricted data requires:

   - A signed agreement covering confidentiality and, where
     applicable, data processing terms
   - Review by the Data Protection Office for any sharing
     involving personal data
   - Transmission through approved secure channels; sharing
     via personal email, consumer messaging, or unapproved
     cloud drives is prohibited
   - A documented business purpose recorded in the data
     sharing log

Cross-border transfers of personal data are subject to
additional safeguards including Standard Contractual Clauses
or equivalent mechanisms and must be reviewed by Legal before
initiation.

================================================================
8. REMOTE WORK PRIVACY RULES
================================================================

Employees working remotely or on a hybrid basis, as governed
by the Remote Work Policy (HR-POL-0061), must take reasonable
steps to protect Confidential and Restricted data in the home
environment:

   - Conduct calls involving customer or employee personal
     data in a private space where conversations cannot be
     overheard by household members or visitors
   - Position screens so that they are not visible to others
   - Do not print Confidential or Restricted data at home
     without prior written approval from both the data owner
     and the Data Protection Office; approved printed
     materials must be stored in a locked location and
     destroyed via cross-cut shredder or returned to a
     Company facility for secure destruction
   - Do not allow household members or visitors to use
     Company devices

================================================================
9. PERSONAL DEVICE RESTRICTIONS
================================================================

Personal devices may access only those Company systems
explicitly authorized through the IT security approval process
described in the IT Security Policy (IT-POL-0203, Section 7)
and the Device Usage Policy (IT-POL-0211). Even when a
personal device is approved for limited access, the following
restrictions apply:

   - Customer data must not be stored locally on the device
   - Confidential documents must not be downloaded to the
     device file system
   - Screenshots of Confidential or Restricted data are
     prohibited
   - Use of personal cloud backup services that may capture
     Company data is prohibited

================================================================
10. INCIDENT REPORTING EXPECTATIONS
================================================================

A privacy incident is any event involving the actual or
suspected unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss,
or destruction of personal or Confidential data. Examples
include misdirected emails containing personal data, lost
printed reports, accidental sharing of customer data with
unauthorized parties, and unauthorized data exports. Privacy
incidents must be reported to the Data Protection Office
within 24 hours of discovery via the Privacy Incident Portal.
Incidents that also constitute security incidents, including
lost devices and suspected account compromise, must
additionally be reported to the Security Operations Center
within 1 hour in accordance with the IT Security Policy
(IT-POL-0203, Section 10) and the Incident Reporting SOP
(SOC-SOP-0418). The Data Protection Office is responsible for
assessing regulatory notification obligations, which in many
jurisdictions require notification to authorities within 72
hours.

================================================================
11. EXCEPTIONS
================================================================

Exceptions to this policy may be granted only where a
documented business need exists and compensating controls are
in place. All exceptions must be requested through the Privacy
Exception process and approved jointly by the Data Protection
Officer and the General Counsel. Exceptions are time-bound,
reviewed at least annually, and do not establish precedent.
