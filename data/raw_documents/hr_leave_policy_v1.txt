1. PURPOSE
================================================================

This policy establishes the standards, entitlements, and
procedures governing all forms of employee leave at Northstar
Analytics Group ("Northstar" or "the Company"). It is intended
to support employee well-being, ensure operational continuity,
and provide a consistent framework for the request, approval,
and documentation of time away from work. All managers and
employees are expected to apply this policy fairly and
consistently across departments.

================================================================
2. SCOPE
================================================================

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time regular
employees of Northstar Analytics Group across all global
offices, including remote employees. Contractors, interns, and
temporary workers are governed by separate agreements and are
not covered under this policy unless explicitly stated in their
engagement terms. Regional statutory entitlements supersede this
policy where local law provides greater benefits.

================================================================
3. PAID TIME OFF (PTO) RULES
================================================================

Full-time employees accrue paid time off at a rate of 1.67 days
per month, totaling 20 business days per calendar year. Part-time
employees accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis according to scheduled
hours. Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days
into the following calendar year; any excess balance will be
forfeited on December 31. PTO requests of 3 or more consecutive
business days require manager approval submitted at least 10
business days in advance through the Workday Absence Module.
PTO requests during critical project periods, including quarter
close, product launches, and client engagement milestones, may
require additional review by the department head. Employees may
not maintain a negative PTO balance except in cases approved by
Human Resources.

================================================================
4. SICK LEAVE RULES
================================================================

Employees are entitled to 10 paid sick days per calendar year,
available from the first day of employment and non-accruing.
Sick leave is intended for personal illness, injury, medical
appointments, and the care of immediate family members who are
ill. Employees must notify their direct manager before the start
of their scheduled workday whenever practical. Sick leave
absences longer than 3 consecutive business days may require
supporting documentation from a licensed medical practitioner,
to be submitted to Human Resources within 5 business days of
return. Unused sick leave does not carry over to the following
year and is not eligible for payout upon separation.

================================================================
5. FAMILY LEAVE RULES
================================================================

Northstar provides up to 16 weeks of paid parental leave to
primary caregivers and 8 weeks to secondary caregivers following
the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. Eligibility
begins after 6 months of continuous employment. Family medical
leave for the serious health condition of a spouse, child, or
parent is available for up to 12 weeks per rolling 12-month
period, in accordance with applicable federal and state law.
Employees must provide at least 30 days advance notice for
foreseeable family leave and submit appropriate certification
forms to Human Resources. Benefits continuation during family
leave follows the standard employer-contribution structure.

================================================================
6. BEREAVEMENT LEAVE RULES
================================================================

Employees are entitled to up to 5 paid bereavement days for the
death of an immediate family member, defined as spouse, domestic
partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, or in-law of
equivalent relationship. Up to 2 paid days are provided for the
death of an extended family member or close friend. Additional
unpaid leave may be requested through the employee's manager and
Human Resources. Documentation is not routinely required but may
be requested in the case of extended absences.

================================================================
7. UNPAID LEAVE RULES
================================================================

Employees who have exhausted other applicable leave categories
may request unpaid leave of absence for personal, educational,
or medical reasons. Unpaid leave requests must be submitted at
least 30 days in advance, except in emergencies, and require
approval from both the direct manager and the department head.
Unpaid leave exceeding 30 calendar days requires additional
approval from the Director of People Operations. Health benefits
may continue during unpaid leave subject to employee premium
contributions. PTO does not accrue during periods of unpaid
leave.

================================================================
8. MANAGER APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS
================================================================

All leave requests must be submitted through the Workday Absence
Module and routed to the employee's direct manager for review.
Managers are expected to respond to leave requests within 3
business days. PTO requests of 3 or more consecutive business
days require explicit manager approval. Leave requests
overlapping with critical project periods, audit cycles, or peak
business seasons may be escalated to the department head for
secondary review. Emergency leave may be approved retroactively
by the manager upon the employee's return, provided notification
was made as soon as reasonably possible.

================================================================
9. NOTICE PERIOD REQUIREMENTS
================================================================

Standard notice requirements are as follows: PTO of 1-2 days
requires 3 business days notice; PTO of 3-5 days requires 10
business days notice; PTO exceeding 5 days requires 20 business
days notice. Family and unpaid leave require 30 calendar days
notice when foreseeable. Sick and bereavement leave require
notification as soon as reasonably practical.

================================================================
10. DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
================================================================

Supporting documentation may be required for: sick leave
exceeding 3 consecutive business days, all family medical leave
requests, accommodations under the Americans with Disabilities
Act, and any leave extending beyond 10 consecutive business
days. Documentation must be submitted to Human Resources, not
the direct manager, to preserve medical privacy.

================================================================
11. EXCEPTIONS
================================================================

Exceptions to this policy may be granted in cases of significant
personal hardship, statutory requirements, or business need.
All exceptions must be documented in writing and approved by the
Director of People Operations. Exceptions do not constitute a
precedent or modification of this policy.
