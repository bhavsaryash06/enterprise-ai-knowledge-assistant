1. PURPOSE
================================================================

This policy defines the standards governing business travel
booked or expensed at Northstar Analytics Group ("Northstar"
or "the Company"). It is a companion policy to the Expense
and Reimbursement Policy (FIN-POL-0117) and provides the
detailed rules that apply specifically to travel-related
expenses. Where this policy and FIN-POL-0117 address the same
topic, the more specific provision of this policy controls
for travel; for all non-travel expenses, FIN-POL-0117
controls.

================================================================
2. SCOPE
================================================================

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time regular
employees of Northstar who travel for business purposes,
including domestic and international travel, single-day
trips, and multi-day engagements. Contractors and temporary
workers are governed by their engagement agreements unless
otherwise stated. Local statutory requirements and customer
contractual travel terms supersede this policy where they
impose stricter or more specific rules.

================================================================
3. BUSINESS TRAVEL APPROVAL
================================================================

All business travel must be pre-approved by the employee's
direct manager before any bookings are made. Approval
requests are submitted through the Concur Travel module and
must include:

   - Business purpose and expected outcomes
   - Travel dates and destinations
   - Estimated total cost, including airfare, lodging,
     ground transportation, and meals
   - Any customer or partner counterparties involved

Travel expected to exceed USD 2,500 in total cost requires
department head approval in addition to manager approval.
International travel additionally requires review by Travel
Operations for visa, insurance, and duty-of-care purposes,
and must be submitted at least 21 calendar days in advance
where practical. Bookings made before approval is granted are
made at the employee's own risk and may not be reimbursable.

================================================================
4. AIRFARE RULES
================================================================

Airfare must be booked through the Company's designated
travel platform whenever possible to ensure rate compliance,
duty-of-care tracking, and policy enforcement. The following
rules apply:

   - Economy class is the standard for all flights regardless
     of duration
   - Premium economy may be selected for flights with
     scheduled flying time exceeding 6 hours, at the
     traveler's discretion
   - Business class is permitted only for flights with
     scheduled flying time exceeding 8 hours and requires
     department head approval
   - First class is not reimbursable under any circumstances
   - Travelers should select the lowest reasonable fare,
     considering total trip cost and scheduling constraints,
     not solely the cheapest available ticket
   - Personal travel may be combined with business travel
     provided the Company pays no more than it would for the
     business-only itinerary; the cost comparison must be
     documented at the time of booking

Changes or cancellations for personal reasons are not
reimbursable.

================================================================
5. HOTEL RULES
================================================================

Lodging must be booked at standard business-class properties
within established city rate caps published in the Travel
Rate Schedule. The following rules apply:

   - Travelers should select rooms at or below the city rate
     cap; rates exceeding the cap require manager approval
     and written justification
   - Room upgrades, in-room entertainment, spa services,
     mini-bar charges, and similar discretionary items are
     not reimbursable
   - In-room internet, parking, and reasonable laundry
     services for trips exceeding 5 nights are reimbursable
   - Personal lodging arrangements, including stays with
     friends or family, are not reimbursable, though
     reasonable courtesy gifts up to USD 50 may be expensed
     in lieu of hotel costs
   - Cancellation fees due to schedule changes initiated by
     the Company or the customer are reimbursable;
     cancellations for personal reasons are not

================================================================
6. MEAL REIMBURSEMENT
================================================================

Meal expenses are reimbursable up to the following daily
limits, inclusive of tax and gratuity, when the traveler is
away from their home location on business:

   - Breakfast:  up to USD 25
   - Lunch:      up to USD 30
   - Dinner:     up to USD 60
   - Daily cap:  USD 115

For international travel, regional adjustments to these
limits are published in the Travel Rate Schedule. Meals
provided by the conference, hotel, or customer are not
separately reimbursable. Group meals where the traveler hosts
colleagues or customers follow the per-attendee limits above
unless the engagement qualifies as approved client
entertainment.

Alcohol is not reimbursable as a meal expense under any
circumstances. Limited alcohol may be reimbursable as part of
pre-approved client entertainment, subject to the rules in
FIN-POL-0117.

================================================================
7. GROUND TRANSPORTATION
================================================================

Reimbursable ground transportation includes airport transfers,
taxis, ride-share services, public transit, rental cars, and
mileage for personal vehicle use on business trips. The
following rules apply:

   - Standard ride-share categories are reimbursable; premium
     or luxury tiers are not reimbursable except where
     standard service is unavailable and the situation is
     documented
   - Rental cars are limited to mid-size or smaller; larger
     vehicles require manager approval and a documented
     business reason such as group travel or equipment
     transport
   - Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursable at the
     prevailing IRS or equivalent regional rate published in
     the Travel Rate Schedule
   - Chauffeur services and private car services are not
     reimbursable
   - Daily commuting expenses to the employee's regular work
     location are not reimbursable

================================================================
8. RECEIPT REQUIREMENTS
================================================================

Itemized receipts are required for all travel expenses of USD
25 or more, consistent with FIN-POL-0117 Section 7. Receipts
must show vendor, date, itemized charges, payment method, and
total. Hotel folios must be itemized and clearly identify any
non-reimbursable charges. Missing receipts require a written
explanation submitted as part of the expense claim and
require Finance Operations approval; the Missing Receipt
Affidavit referenced in FIN-POL-0117 is limited to two
occurrences per employee per calendar year and counts toward
the same limit regardless of expense type.

================================================================
9. EXPENSE SUBMISSION DEADLINES
================================================================

Travel expenses must be submitted through the Concur Expense
system within 30 calendar days of trip completion. Claims
submitted between 31 and 60 days require written
justification and department head approval. Claims submitted
beyond 60 days will not be reimbursed except in documented
exception cases. End-of-fiscal-year travel completed in
December must be submitted no later than January 10 of the
following year. Travelers who anticipate delayed submission
due to extended travel or absence should consult their
manager and Finance Operations in advance.

================================================================
10. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
================================================================

In addition to the items identified throughout this policy
and in FIN-POL-0117 Section 4, the following travel-related
expenses are not reimbursable:

   - First-class airfare and unauthorized cabin upgrades
   - Luxury or premium-tier ride-share, rental, or
     chauffeured services
   - Alcohol outside of pre-approved client entertainment
   - Personal travel insurance beyond the Company-provided
     coverage
   - Travel expenses for spouses, partners, family members,
     or unaccompanied minors
   - Lost or damaged personal items, including baggage not
     covered by airline reimbursement
   - Traffic citations, parking violations, and toll
     evasion charges
   - In-flight purchases unrelated to business work, including
     entertainment packages and duty-free goods

================================================================
11. APPROVAL LIMITS
================================================================

Travel expense approval authority follows the structure
defined in FIN-POL-0117 Section 8:

   - Up to USD 500:            Direct manager
   - USD 501 to USD 2,500:     Department head
   - USD 2,501 to USD 10,000:  Vice President of Finance
   - Over USD 10,000:          Chief Financial Officer

Approval thresholds apply to total trip cost as well as
individual expense claims. Splitting a single trip into
multiple claims to remain below an approval threshold is
prohibited.

================================================================
12. EXCEPTIONS
================================================================

Exceptions to this policy may be granted in cases of
documented business necessity, medical accommodation, or
extraordinary circumstances such as flight disruptions and
safety concerns. All exceptions must be requested in writing
and approved by the Vice President of Finance. Exceptions
are recorded in the Finance exception log and do not
establish precedent.
