1. PURPOSE
================================================================

This Standard Operating Procedure ("SOP") defines how
incidents are identified, reported, triaged, and escalated at
Northstar Analytics Group ("Northstar" or "the Company"). It
provides a single, consistent reporting path for security,
privacy, and workplace incidents, ensuring that the
appropriate response functions are engaged promptly and that
obligations under the IT Security Policy (IT-POL-0203), Data
Privacy Policy (DPO-POL-0309), Access Control Policy
(IT-POL-0224), and Device Usage Policy (IT-POL-0211) are met.

================================================================
2. SCOPE
================================================================

This SOP applies to all employees, contractors, interns, and
third parties who work for or on behalf of Northstar,
regardless of location or device. It covers incidents
affecting Company information systems, data assets, personnel,
facilities, and supporting infrastructure. Local statutory or
regulatory reporting requirements supersede this SOP where
they impose stricter or faster obligations.

================================================================
3. WHAT COUNTS AS AN INCIDENT
================================================================

An incident is any unplanned event that has caused, or has the
potential to cause, harm to Northstar's people, customers,
data, systems, or facilities. Incidents are categorized into
three types for routing purposes:

   - Security incident:  Affects the confidentiality,
                         integrity, or availability of Company
                         information systems
   - Privacy incident:   Involves the unauthorized access,
                         disclosure, alteration, or loss of
                         personal or Confidential data
   - Workplace incident: Affects the physical safety,
                         well-being, or working conditions of
                         personnel or visitors

An event may fall into more than one category, in which case
all applicable reporting paths must be followed
simultaneously. When in doubt, employees should report the
event; under-reporting is treated as a more serious concern
than over-reporting.

================================================================
4. SECURITY INCIDENT EXAMPLES
================================================================

Examples of reportable security incidents include, but are
not limited to:

   - Lost or stolen Company device (laptop, phone, tablet,
     hardware security key, or removable media)
   - Suspected account compromise (unexpected MFA prompts,
     unrecognized login locations, password change emails
     not initiated by the user)
   - Malware infection or suspicious software behavior on a
     Company device
   - Unauthorized physical access to a Company facility or
     restricted area
   - Unauthorized configuration changes to Company systems
   - Discovery of unapproved software or unauthorized network
     equipment in use

================================================================
5. PRIVACY INCIDENT EXAMPLES
================================================================

Examples of reportable privacy incidents include, but are not
limited to:

   - Email containing personal or customer data sent to the
     wrong recipient
   - Loss of a printed Confidential or Restricted document
   - Accidental sharing of customer reports with an
     unauthorized internal or external party
   - Unauthorized export, download, or screenshot of customer
     data
   - Discovery that personal data has been stored on a
     personal device, personal cloud service, or personal
     email account
   - Receipt of a data subject complaint alleging mishandling
     of personal information

================================================================
6. WORKPLACE INCIDENT EXAMPLES
================================================================

Examples of reportable workplace incidents include, but are
not limited to:

   - Injury or near-miss at a Company facility or while
     performing Company business
   - Injury sustained by a remote employee during scheduled
     work hours
   - Property damage, fire, flood, or environmental hazard
     affecting a Company facility
   - Threats, harassment, or violence directed at personnel
     or visitors
   - Medical emergencies on Company premises
   - Building security failures (propped doors, tailgating,
     malfunctioning access controls)

================================================================
7. REPORTING TIMELINES
================================================================

All incidents must be reported using the following timelines,
measured from the moment of discovery:

   - Lost or stolen Company device:        Immediately, no
                                            later than 1 hour
   - Active or in-progress cyber incident: Immediately, no
                                            later than 1 hour
   - Workplace incident involving injury:  Immediately, no
                                            later than 1 hour
   - All other security incidents:         Within 24 hours
   - Privacy incidents:                    Within 24 hours
   - Non-injury workplace incidents:       Within 24 hours

Regulatory notification clocks for personal data breaches,
which in many jurisdictions require notification to
authorities within 72 hours, are managed by the Data
Protection Office in accordance with DPO-POL-0309. Employees
are not responsible for assessing regulatory obligations;
their responsibility is to report promptly.

================================================================
8. REPORTING CHANNELS
================================================================

Incidents must be reported through the channel corresponding
to their primary category. Where an incident spans categories,
report through the most urgent channel first and rely on the
intake teams to engage the others.

   - Security incidents:  Security Operations Center (SOC)
                          Hotline: +1 (415) 555-0911
                          Portal:  security.northstaranalytics.internal
   - Privacy incidents:   Data Protection Office
                          Email:   privacy@northstaranalytics.com
                          Portal:  privacy.northstaranalytics.internal
   - Workplace incidents: People Operations Service Desk and
                          Workplace Operations
                          Email:   incidents@northstaranalytics.com
                          Phone:   +1 (415) 555-0188

Employees must not investigate suspected cyber incidents on
their own. Do not click suspicious links to "test" them, do
not log in to suspected phishing pages, do not run unfamiliar
files, and do not attempt to remove malware from a device.
Unauthorized investigation can destroy forensic evidence and
expand the scope of an incident.

================================================================
9. MANAGER RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================

Managers who are notified of an incident by a team member
must:

   - Confirm that the incident has been logged through the
     appropriate channel; if not, assist the employee in
     submitting the report immediately
   - Refrain from instructing the employee to delay reporting
     for any reason, including pending internal review
   - Support the employee through the response process and
     ensure they are not penalized for good-faith reporting
   - Engage their own management chain for incidents that may
     affect customer commitments or public reputation

Managers may not close, dismiss, or downgrade incidents
without authorization from the relevant intake function.

================================================================
10. IT RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================

The Security Operations Center is responsible for the intake,
triage, containment, eradication, and recovery phases of
security incidents in accordance with the IT Security Policy
(IT-POL-0203). IT responsibilities include:

   - Acknowledging incident reports within 30 minutes for
     Severity 1 and 2 incidents and within 4 hours for lower
     severities
   - Preserving forensic evidence and maintaining chain of
     custody
   - Coordinating with the Data Protection Office where
     personal data is involved
   - Coordinating with Access Control administrators
     (IT-POL-0224) to revoke or rotate credentials as needed
   - Producing a post-incident review for all Severity 1 and
     2 incidents within 10 business days of closure

================================================================
11. HR RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================

People Operations is responsible for the response to
workplace incidents, in coordination with Workplace
Operations for facility-related matters. HR responsibilities
include:

   - Ensuring injured employees receive appropriate medical
     attention and managing any workers' compensation process
   - Supporting employees affected by harassment, violence,
     or psychological harm, including access to the Employee
     Assistance Program
   - Coordinating with Legal where incidents may have
     regulatory or litigation implications
   - Maintaining incident records in accordance with
     statutory retention requirements

================================================================
12. ESCALATION PROCESS
================================================================

Incidents are escalated based on severity and potential
impact:

   - Severity 3 (Low):      Handled by intake function;
                            standard reporting
   - Severity 2 (Medium):   Director-level notification
                            within 4 hours
   - Severity 1 (High):     Executive notification within 1
                            hour; cross-functional incident
                            response team convened
   - Severity 0 (Critical): Immediate CEO and Board
                            notification; external counsel
                            and communications engaged

Privacy incidents involving customer data are escalated to
the Compliance function regardless of severity, for
assessment against contractual and regulatory obligations.
Cross-functional response is led by the on-call Incident
Commander assigned by the Security Operations Center.
