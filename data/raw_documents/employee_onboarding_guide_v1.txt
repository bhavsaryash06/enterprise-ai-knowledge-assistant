1. PURPOSE
================================================================

This guide defines the standard process for onboarding new
hires at Northstar Analytics Group ("Northstar" or "the
Company"). It establishes a consistent experience across
departments and locations, ensures that new employees are
productive and compliant from their start date, and aligns
onboarding activities with the Company's security, privacy,
and access control commitments. It is intended for use by
hiring managers, People Operations partners, IT, and new
hires themselves.

================================================================
2. SCOPE
================================================================

This guide applies to all full-time and part-time regular
employees joining Northstar in any global office, including
fully remote and hybrid hires. Contractor and intern
onboarding follows a parallel process documented separately
by Talent Operations. Regional onboarding addenda may
supplement this guide where local statutory requirements
apply.

================================================================
3. PRE-BOARDING STEPS
================================================================

Pre-boarding begins once an offer letter is accepted and
continues through the day before the start date. During this
period:

   - Talent Acquisition issues the offer letter, background
     check authorization, and pre-boarding portal access
   - People Operations creates the employee record in Workday
     and triggers downstream provisioning workflows
   - The hiring manager submits the System Access Request
     (see Section 7) at least 10 business days before the
     start date
   - IT provisions Company equipment so that it is assigned
     and ready before the employee starts work; remote hires
     receive equipment via prepaid logistics with delivery
     confirmation at least 2 business days before start
   - The hiring manager prepares a 30/60/90-day plan and
     identifies an onboarding buddy
   - For office-based hires, Workplace Operations issues a
     building access badge in accordance with IT-POL-0224

================================================================
4. FIRST DAY TASKS
================================================================

The new hire's first day focuses on welcome, basic
provisioning, and the start of compliance activities. Standard
first-day items include:

   - Welcome session with the hiring manager and onboarding
     buddy
   - Workday self-service login and initial profile review
   - Activation of Company laptop, single sign-on credentials,
     and the multi-factor authentication enrollment process
     in accordance with the IT Security Policy (IT-POL-0203,
     Section 4)
   - Acknowledgement of the Code of Conduct and the
     Acceptable Use Statement
   - Introduction to the team and review of working norms,
     including hybrid attendance expectations under the
     Remote Work Policy (HR-POL-0061)
   - Review of the new hire's 30/60/90-day plan with the
     manager

The first day should not include access to customer data,
production systems, or any Restricted information, regardless
of role.

================================================================
5. FIRST WEEK TASKS
================================================================

By the end of the first week, the new hire should have
completed:

   - All mandatory HR documentation (see Section 9)
   - Security Awareness training (see Section 10)
   - Initial role-specific system access activation through
     single sign-on
   - Manager-led walkthrough of team tools, repositories, and
     documentation
   - Introductory meetings with at least three
     cross-functional partners identified by the hiring
     manager
   - Completion of the new-hire feedback survey on Day 5

Customer-facing or data-handling roles should additionally
complete the Data Privacy Foundations module before being
granted access to any customer data, as required by
DPO-POL-0309.

================================================================
6. REQUIRED TRAINING
================================================================

All new hires must complete the following training modules
during their first 30 days:

   - Security Awareness: Within 5 business days of start
   - Code of Conduct and Ethics: Within 10 business days
   - Data Privacy Foundations: Within 10 business days
   - Anti-Harassment and Inclusion: Within 30 days
   - Incident Reporting Overview (SOC-SOP-0418): Within 30
     days
   - Role-specific compliance modules: As assigned by the
     hiring manager

Training completion is tracked in the Learning Management
System. Failure to complete Security Awareness within 5
business days results in automatic suspension of system
access pending completion, as required by IT-POL-0203.

================================================================
7. SYSTEM ACCESS REQUESTS
================================================================

System access must be requested by the hiring manager through
the Access Request Portal, not by the new hire. Access is
granted on the basis of role, demonstrated business need, and
the principle of least privilege as defined in the Access
Control Policy (IT-POL-0224). Standard role templates exist
for common positions and should be used wherever possible to
reduce provisioning errors.

   - Standard access requests are processed within 3 business
     days
   - Access to Confidential or Restricted systems requires
     data owner approval and may take up to 5 business days
   - Privileged access (administrator, infrastructure, or
     production) requires CISO-delegated approval and is
     granted only after completion of role-specific privileged
     access training

Access provisioned before the start date is held in an
inactive state and activated automatically on Day 1.

================================================================
8. EQUIPMENT SETUP
================================================================

Company equipment must be assigned before the employee starts
work. Standard issue includes a Company laptop, charger,
headset, and hardware security key. Hybrid and remote hires
receive equipment by prepaid logistics with tracking provided
to the hiring manager and the new hire. All issued equipment
is recorded in the asset management system in accordance with
the Device Usage Policy (IT-POL-0211). Home office equipment
reimbursement requests are governed by the Expense and
Reimbursement Policy and require manager approval before
purchase. New hires must not connect personal peripherals to
Company systems unless those peripherals were purchased from
an approved vendor or have received individual IT approval as
described in IT-POL-0203, Section 6.

================================================================
9. HR DOCUMENTATION
================================================================

HR forms must be completed within the first 3 business days.
Required documentation includes:

   - Employment eligibility verification (I-9 or regional
     equivalent)
   - Tax withholding forms
   - Direct deposit and payroll setup
   - Benefits enrollment elections (or deferral to the open
     enrollment window)
   - Emergency contact information
   - Confidentiality and intellectual property agreements
   - Acknowledgement of the Employee Handbook and core
     policies

Forms are submitted through the Workday self-service portal.
People Operations reviews submissions within 2 business days
and contacts the new hire for any required corrections.

================================================================
10. SECURITY TRAINING
================================================================

Security Awareness training is a mandatory module covering
phishing recognition and reporting, password and MFA hygiene,
device security, data classification, and incident reporting.
The training must be completed within 5 business days of the
start date. Completion is a prerequisite for continued access
to Company systems beyond the initial provisioning window;
incomplete training triggers automatic access suspension
managed by the Security Operations Center.

Role-specific security training is assigned based on system
access; for example, employees granted privileged access
complete additional modules before access is activated.

================================================================
11. MANAGER RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================

Hiring managers are accountable for the success of the
onboarding experience. Manager responsibilities include:

   - Submitting the System Access Request at least 10
     business days before the start date
   - Confirming equipment delivery for remote hires
   - Preparing the 30/60/90-day plan and assigning an
     onboarding buddy
   - Holding daily check-ins during the first week and
     weekly check-ins through Day 90
   - Monitoring training completion and intervening promptly
     where deadlines are at risk
   - Confirming role-appropriate access following
     least-privilege principles and removing any access that
     is not required

================================================================
12. NEW HIRE RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================

New hires are responsible for actively engaging in the
onboarding process. New hire responsibilities include:

   - Completing all required HR documentation within 3
     business days
   - Completing Security Awareness training within 5 business
     days
   - Completing all other required training within assigned
     deadlines
   - Safeguarding Company equipment and credentials from the
     moment of receipt
   - Reporting any onboarding issues, missing access, or
     security concerns to the hiring manager and the
     appropriate intake function under SOC-SOP-0418
   - Asking questions early; onboarding is the best time to
     clarify expectations
