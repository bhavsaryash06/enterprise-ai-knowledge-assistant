1. PURPOSE
================================================================

This policy establishes the standards governing the request,
approval, provisioning, review, and removal of access to
Northstar Analytics Group ("Northstar" or "the Company")
systems, applications, data, and facilities. It is intended
to ensure that access is granted only to authorized
individuals, is appropriate to their role, is regularly
reviewed, and is removed promptly when no longer required.
This policy implements the access-related obligations
referenced in the IT Security Policy (IT-POL-0203) and the
Data Privacy Policy (DPO-POL-0309).

================================================================
2. SCOPE
================================================================

This policy applies to all access granted to Northstar
systems and facilities, regardless of the requester's
employment classification, location, or device. It covers
employees, contractors, interns, vendors, auditors, and any
third party requiring access. It governs logical access to
information systems and physical access to Company
facilities and secure zones. Local statutory or regulatory
requirements supersede this policy where they impose
stricter controls.

================================================================
3. ACCESS REQUEST PROCESS
================================================================

All access requests must be submitted through the Access
Request Portal. Self-requested access is permitted only for
the activation of pre-approved role templates assigned at
onboarding (HR-GDE-0074, Section 7); all other access
requests must be submitted by the requester's manager on
their behalf. Each request must include:

   - The specific system, application, dataset, or facility
   - The level of access required (read, write, administer,
     or equivalent)
   - The business justification
   - The expected duration, where time-bound

Standard requests are processed within 3 business days.
Requests for Confidential or Restricted data, as defined in
DPO-POL-0309 Section 3, require additional data owner
approval and may take up to 5 business days. Requests made
outside this process, including peer-to-peer credential
sharing or undocumented access grants, are prohibited and
treated as a security incident under SOC-SOP-0418.

================================================================
4. LEAST PRIVILEGE PRINCIPLE
================================================================

Access at Northstar is governed by the principle of least
privilege: individuals are granted the minimum access
required to perform their assigned duties, and no more.
Specific applications of this principle include:

   - Access is scoped to the smallest viable data set, system
     component, or facility zone
   - Time-bound access is preferred where the underlying need
     is project-based, seasonal, or short-term
   - Write and administrative permissions are granted only
     where read access is insufficient for the role
   - Access is reduced or revoked promptly when a role change,
     project conclusion, or extended leave reduces the
     business need

Managers are accountable for ensuring that the access held by
their team members remains aligned with current
responsibilities.

================================================================
5. MANAGER APPROVAL RULES
================================================================

All access must be approved by the requester's direct
manager. Managers approve access by attesting that:

   - The requested access is consistent with the requester's
     current role and responsibilities
   - The least-privilege principle has been applied
   - The requester has completed any prerequisite training
     required for the access being requested

Managers may not approve their own access requests; such
requests are routed automatically to the next level of
management. Approval is not a one-time act: managers carry
ongoing accountability for the appropriateness of access
held by their team members and are required to participate
in the periodic access reviews described in Section 7.

================================================================
6. ROLE-BASED ACCESS
================================================================

Northstar uses role-based access control (RBAC) as the
primary access model. Role templates are defined and
maintained by the Identity and Access Management team in
coordination with data owners and business stakeholders.
Standard roles are used wherever possible to reduce
provisioning errors, simplify reviews, and accelerate
onboarding.

Privileged roles, including administrator access to
production infrastructure, source code management, customer
data stores, identity providers, and security tooling,
require explicit approval by IT Security in addition to
manager approval. Privileged access is granted only after
completion of role-specific privileged access training,
enforcement of hardware-key multi-factor authentication, and
acknowledgment of just-in-time access procedures where
applicable.

Service accounts and other non-human identities are governed
under the same approval framework as user accounts. Each
service account must have a documented owner, a defined
business purpose, scoped permissions, and rotated
credentials in line with the IT Security Policy
(IT-POL-0203, Section 3). Use of shared interactive accounts
by multiple individuals is prohibited.

================================================================
7. ACCESS REVIEW FREQUENCY
================================================================

Access is reviewed on a recurring basis to ensure ongoing
alignment with business need:

   - Standard user access: Reviewed every 90 days by the
     requester's manager and the system data owner
   - Privileged access: Reviewed every 30 days by IT
     Security and the system data owner
   - Service accounts: Reviewed every 90 days by the
     documented owner
   - Facility access to secure zones: Reviewed every 90 days
     by Workplace Operations

Reviews are conducted through the Access Governance Portal.
Reviewers must either reaffirm or revoke each access entry;
inaction past the review deadline results in automatic
revocation of the reviewed access. Patterns of non-completion
are escalated to department heads and tracked as a
compliance metric.

================================================================
8. TERMINATION ACCESS REMOVAL
================================================================

Access for terminated employees must be removed within 24
hours of the official separation time recorded in Workday.
For voluntary separations with advance notice, deprovisioning
is scheduled to execute at the close of the final working
day. For involuntary separations, deprovisioning is executed
immediately upon notification from People Operations, and may
precede the employee's notification at the discretion of the
business.

Deprovisioning includes disabling user accounts, revoking
single sign-on access, rotating any shared credentials the
employee may have known, returning hardware security keys,
and removing physical access badges. Mailbox and file access
held by the departing employee is transferred to the manager
or a designated successor in accordance with the Data Privacy
Policy (DPO-POL-0309).

================================================================
9. CONTRACTOR ACCESS RULES
================================================================

Contractor and third-party access is subject to additional
controls reflecting the time-bound nature of these
engagements. All contractor access must:

   - Have a defined expiration date no later than the end of
     the contracted engagement, with a hard maximum of 12
     months before re-approval is required
   - Be sponsored by an employee who acts as the
     engagement's accountable owner
   - Be limited to the systems and data necessary for the
     contracted work, applying least-privilege strictly
   - Be revoked automatically upon expiration unless an
     extension is approved before the expiration date

Contractor access to Confidential or Restricted data
additionally requires a signed confidentiality and data
processing agreement on file with Legal before access is
provisioned.

================================================================
10. EMERGENCY ACCESS
================================================================

Emergency access may be granted to address production
incidents, security incidents, or other time-critical
business situations where the standard approval timeline
cannot be met. Emergency access:

   - Is granted only by IT Security or a delegated on-call
     authority
   - Is time-bound, with a default duration of 4 hours and a
     maximum of 24 hours before re-approval is required
   - Is fully logged, including the requester, the granting
     authority, the systems accessed, and the actions
     performed
   - Is reviewed within 5 business days by IT Security and
     the relevant data owner, with any access not justified
     in retrospect documented as a finding

Routine or repeated use of the emergency access path is
treated as a process failure and triggers review of the
underlying role design.

================================================================
11. EXCEPTIONS
================================================================

Exceptions to this policy may be granted only where a
documented business need exists and compensating controls
are in place. All exceptions must be requested through the
Security Exception Management process and approved jointly
by the CISO and the Director of Identity and Access
Management. Exceptions are time-bound, do not exceed 12
months without re-review, and do not establish precedent.
