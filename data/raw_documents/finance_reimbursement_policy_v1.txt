1. PURPOSE
================================================================

This policy defines the standards under which Northstar
Analytics Group ("Northstar" or "the Company") reimburses
employees for business-related expenses incurred in the course
of their duties. It is intended to provide a consistent,
auditable, and equitable framework for expense submission,
approval, and payment, while ensuring fiscal responsibility and
compliance with applicable tax and accounting requirements.
This policy works in conjunction with the Remote Work Policy
(HR-POL-0061) for home office provisioning and with the IT
Security Policy (IT-POL-0203) for any equipment connected to
Company systems.

================================================================
2. SCOPE
================================================================

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time regular
employees of Northstar Analytics Group across all global
offices, including fully remote and hybrid employees.
Contractors and temporary workers are reimbursed in accordance
with their engagement agreements and are not covered by this
policy. Where local statutory or tax requirements provide
greater employee protection, those requirements take
precedence.

================================================================
3. ELIGIBLE EXPENSES
================================================================

The following categories of expenses are eligible for
reimbursement when incurred for a legitimate business purpose
and supported by appropriate documentation: business travel
including airfare in economy class, ground transportation,
lodging at standard business-class properties, and per-diem
meals; client entertainment within published per-person limits;
professional development including pre-approved training,
certifications, and conferences; mobile phone and home internet
stipends for eligible remote employees as defined in
HR-POL-0061; home office equipment within the limits set in
Section 5; software subscriptions purchased through the
Company's approved procurement channels; and business supplies
required for the performance of assigned duties.

================================================================
4. NON-ELIGIBLE EXPENSES
================================================================

The following expenses are not reimbursable under any
circumstances: personal entertainment including streaming
services, gaming subscriptions, and recreational memberships;
luxury upgrades on travel, including first-class airfare,
premium lodging upgrades, and chauffeur services; alcohol
purchased outside of approved client entertainment contexts;
traffic violations, parking fines, and personal insurance
deductibles; spouse, partner, or family travel expenses; gifts
to government officials or any expense that may constitute a
conflict of interest; cosmetic or decorative items for the home
office; and any equipment purchased outside of IT-approved
vendor channels where such equipment is intended to connect to
Company systems (see Section 6 and IT-POL-0203).

================================================================
5. HOME OFFICE REIMBURSEMENT RULES
================================================================

Eligible remote and hybrid employees, as defined in the Remote
Work Policy (HR-POL-0061), may submit reimbursement claims for
qualifying home office equipment up to the following limits per
36-month replacement cycle:

   - Monitor:        up to USD 250
   - Office chair:   up to USD 300
   - Desk:           up to USD 400
   - Keyboard/mouse: up to USD 75 combined
   - Headset:        up to USD 100

All home office equipment purchases require manager approval
before purchase. Post-purchase submissions without prior
approval will not be reimbursed except in the documented
exception cases described in Section 12. Employees should
retain ownership records; equipment under USD 500 in unit value
remains the property of the employee, while equipment at or
above this threshold is recorded as a Company asset and must be
returned upon separation.

================================================================
6. EQUIPMENT CONNECTED TO COMPANY SYSTEMS
================================================================

Any device that connects to Northstar networks, accesses
Company data, or integrates with Company-managed software must
be procured through an IT-approved vendor as listed in the
current IT Vendor Catalog (IT-POL-0203, Appendix B). This
includes but is not limited to laptops, external storage,
docking stations, networking hardware, and security
peripherals. Reimbursement claims for such equipment purchased
outside the approved vendor list will be denied, regardless of
prior manager approval. Employees with questions regarding
vendor eligibility should consult IT Procurement before
purchase.

================================================================
7. REQUIRED RECEIPTS
================================================================

Itemized receipts are required for all expenses of USD 25 or
more. Receipts must clearly show the vendor name, transaction
date, itemized purchase details, payment method, and total
amount. Credit card statements alone are not acceptable as
substitute documentation. Receipts must be uploaded in PDF or
high-resolution image format to the Concur Expense system at
the time of claim submission. Lost receipts may be replaced
with a signed Missing Receipt Affidavit, limited to two
occurrences per employee per calendar year.

================================================================
8. APPROVAL LIMITS
================================================================

Approval authority is structured as follows:

   - Up to USD 500:            Direct manager
   - USD 501 to USD 2,500:     Department head
   - USD 2,501 to USD 10,000:  Vice President of Finance
   - Over USD 10,000:          Chief Financial Officer

Expenses exceeding USD 500 require department head approval in
addition to manager approval. Approval thresholds apply to the
total claim, not individual line items; intentional splitting
of claims to remain below an approval threshold is a violation
of this policy and may result in disciplinary action.

================================================================
9. SUBMISSION DEADLINES
================================================================

All reimbursement claims must be submitted through the Concur
Expense system within 30 calendar days of the date the expense
was incurred. Claims submitted between 31 and 60 days require
written justification and department head approval. Claims
submitted beyond 60 days will not be reimbursed except in
documented exception cases. End-of-fiscal-year expenses
incurred in December must be submitted no later than January 10
of the following year to ensure proper accounting period
recognition.

================================================================
10. MANAGER APPROVAL RULES
================================================================

Managers are responsible for reviewing the business purpose,
reasonableness, and policy compliance of all submitted claims.
Managers are expected to act on claims within 5 business days
of submission. Managers may not approve their own expense
claims; such claims are routed automatically to the next level
of management. Approval of a claim certifies that the manager
has reviewed the supporting documentation and confirms the
expense was incurred for legitimate business purposes.

================================================================
11. FINANCE REVIEW PROCESS
================================================================

Following manager approval, the Finance Operations team
conducts a secondary review of all claims for documentation
completeness, policy compliance, and accounting accuracy. The
standard review cycle is 7 business days. Approved claims are
processed in the next bi-weekly reimbursement run via direct
deposit. Finance may request additional documentation or
clarification; claims with outstanding queries are not
processed until resolved. A monthly audit sample of 5% of
processed claims is conducted by Internal Audit.

================================================================
12. EXCEPTIONS
================================================================

Exceptions to this policy may be granted for documented
emergencies, regulatory requirements, or extraordinary business
need. All exceptions must be requested in writing and approved
by the Vice President of Finance. Exceptions are recorded in
the Finance exception log and do not establish precedent.
