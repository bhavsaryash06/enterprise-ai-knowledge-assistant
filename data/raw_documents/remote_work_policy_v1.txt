1. PURPOSE
================================================================

This policy defines the standards, expectations, and approval
processes governing remote and hybrid work arrangements at
Northstar Analytics Group ("Northstar" or "the Company"). It is
intended to support workforce flexibility while maintaining
operational continuity, information security, and equitable
treatment across teams. This policy works in conjunction with
the Expense and Reimbursement Policy (FIN-POL-0117) for home
office provisioning, the IT Security Policy (IT-POL-0203) for
device and network controls, and the Data Privacy Policy
(DPO-POL-0309) for the handling of personal and customer data
outside Company premises.

================================================================
2. SCOPE
================================================================

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time regular
employees of Northstar Analytics Group across all global
offices who perform work outside a designated Company facility,
whether on a fully remote, hybrid, or occasional basis.
Contractors and temporary workers are governed by their
engagement agreements. Local statutory requirements supersede
this policy where they provide greater employee protection.

================================================================
3. REMOTE WORK ELIGIBILITY
================================================================

Eligibility for remote work is determined by role
characteristics, tenure, and performance, not by personal
preference alone. Roles that require on-site presence,
including hardware operations, secure data handling rooms, and
designated client-facing reception functions, are not eligible
for fully remote arrangements. Employees must have completed at
least 90 days of continuous employment and be in good
performance standing to apply for a fully remote arrangement.
Employees must receive written manager approval before
switching from in-office or hybrid status to fully remote
status. Eligibility is reviewed annually and may be revised
based on business need, performance, or restructuring.

================================================================
4. HYBRID WORK EXPECTATIONS
================================================================

The default working model at Northstar is hybrid, with
employees expected to be present in their assigned office a
minimum of 2 days per week, including any team-designated
anchor days. Anchor days are set by department heads and
communicated at least 30 days in advance of any change.
Employees on hybrid arrangements are expected to participate
in-person for: quarterly team planning sessions, new-hire
onboarding weeks, and any client meetings designated as
in-person by the engagement lead. Hybrid employees may not
convert to a fully remote arrangement without going through
the approval process described in Section 5.

================================================================
5. MANAGER APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS
================================================================

All remote and hybrid work arrangements require formal manager
approval recorded in the Workday Employee Profile. The
following changes require explicit, written approval:

   - Initial transition to a hybrid schedule
   - Transition from hybrid to fully remote
   - Change in primary work location across state or
     national borders
   - Temporary remote work exceeding 10 consecutive business
     days
   - Reduction in office presence below the published anchor
     day requirements

Cross-border location changes additionally require review by
Human Resources, Payroll, and Tax Operations before approval,
and may be subject to legal restrictions in certain
jurisdictions. Managers are expected to respond to remote work
requests within 10 business days.

================================================================
6. HOME OFFICE SETUP RULES
================================================================

Employees working remotely or on a hybrid basis are responsible
for establishing a home office environment that supports safe,
productive, and confidential work. Minimum requirements
include: a dedicated work surface, an ergonomic seating
arrangement, adequate lighting, and a quiet space suitable for
virtual meetings and the handling of sensitive information.
Reimbursable equipment categories and limits are defined in the
Expense and Reimbursement Policy (FIN-POL-0117, Section 5).
Home office equipment purchases must be approved by the
employee's manager before purchase; post-purchase claims
without prior approval will not be reimbursed. Employees are
responsible for maintaining a safe home work environment and
must report any work-related injury to Human Resources within
24 hours.

================================================================
7. WORK HOURS AND AVAILABILITY
================================================================

Remote and hybrid employees are expected to maintain their
standard scheduled working hours and remain reachable through
Company-approved communication channels during those hours.
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the
employee's assigned home time zone, during which employees
should be available for synchronous meetings and rapid
response. Employees working across time zones must coordinate
with their manager to establish reasonable overlap windows
with their team. Time-off requests follow the Leave Policy
(HR-POL-0042) regardless of work location.

================================================================
8. EQUIPMENT RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================

Employees are responsible for the safekeeping of all
Company-issued equipment, including laptops, monitors, docking
stations, and peripherals. Equipment must be used solely for
business purposes by the assigned employee and may not be
shared with household members. Employees must maintain
reliable broadband internet access sufficient to support video
conferencing, file transfer, and the use of Company
applications. Internet outages exceeding 4 hours during
scheduled work time must be reported to the employee's manager.
Loss, theft, or damage of Company equipment must be reported
to IT Service Desk within 24 hours. Upon separation, all
Company equipment must be returned within 10 business days
through the prepaid logistics process coordinated by Workplace
Operations.

================================================================
9. SECURITY EXPECTATIONS
================================================================

Remote work environments are subject to the full set of
controls described in the IT Security Policy (IT-POL-0203) and
the Data Privacy Policy (DPO-POL-0309). Specific expectations
include:

   - Use only Company-approved communication and collaboration
     tools for business discussions and file sharing
   - Connect to Company systems exclusively through the
     Company VPN when handling internal or restricted data
   - Lock devices when stepping away, even briefly, and ensure
     screens are not visible to household members or visitors
   - Do not print confidential customer data at home without
     prior written approval from both the data owner and the
     Data Privacy Office
   - Do not store Company data on personal devices or unapproved
     cloud services
   - Report any suspected security incident to the Security
     Operations Center within 1 hour of discovery

Repeated security violations or performance issues materially
attributable to remote work may result in the revocation of
remote work privileges and a required return to an assigned
office location, in addition to any disciplinary measures
applicable under Company policy.

================================================================
10. EXCEPTIONS
================================================================

Exceptions to this policy may be granted for documented
medical, family care, or extraordinary business circumstances.
All exceptions must be requested in writing and approved
jointly by the employee's manager and the Director of People
Operations. Exceptions are recorded in the Workday Employee
Profile and do not establish precedent.
