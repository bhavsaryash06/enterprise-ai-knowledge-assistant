1. PURPOSE
================================================================

This policy defines the standards governing the acceptable
use, configuration, and handling of devices that access
Northstar Analytics Group ("Northstar" or "the Company")
systems or process Company data. It supplements the baseline
security controls established in the IT Security Policy
(IT-POL-0203) with device-specific behavioral expectations
and operational rules.

================================================================
2. SCOPE
================================================================

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, interns,
and third parties who use any device, whether Company-issued
or personally owned, to perform work for Northstar or to
access Northstar systems and data. It covers laptops,
desktops, mobile phones, tablets, removable media, hardware
security keys, and any peripheral that connects to a
Company-managed device or network. Local statutory
requirements supersede this policy where they impose stricter
controls.

================================================================
3. COMPANY DEVICE USAGE
================================================================

Company-issued devices must be used primarily for business
purposes. Incidental personal use is permitted provided it
does not interfere with work, consume material Company
resources, or violate any Company policy. The following are
prohibited on Company devices under any circumstances:

   - Storage or transmission of unlawful, harassing, or
     discriminatory content
   - Storage of personal financial, medical, or government
     identification records belonging to the user or others
   - Use of file-sharing or torrenting clients
   - Cryptocurrency mining or similar resource-intensive
     personal computation
   - Sharing the device with household members, friends, or
     other unauthorized individuals

Employees should have no expectation of privacy in their use
of Company devices. The Company reserves the right to
monitor, audit, and access Company devices in accordance with
applicable law and the Data Privacy Policy (DPO-POL-0309).

================================================================
4. PERSONAL DEVICE USAGE
================================================================

Personal devices may not access Company systems without prior
IT security approval. Approval is granted through the Bring
Your Own Device (BYOD) intake process and requires:

   - Enrollment in the Company mobile device management (MDM)
     platform
   - A device operating system within the supported version
     window
   - Acceptance of the BYOD terms, including consent to
     remote wipe of Company data
   - Confirmation that the device meets the baseline security
     requirements in Section 9

Approved personal devices may access only the specific
systems identified in the approval record. Use of personal
devices for activities not covered by the approval is
prohibited. Approval may be revoked at any time for security,
performance, or policy reasons, and is automatically revoked
upon separation.

================================================================
5. MOBILE DEVICE RULES
================================================================

Mobile devices, whether Company-issued or approved personal,
must comply with the following:

   - Enrollment in the Company MDM platform with the
     Company configuration profile applied
   - Device passcode of at least 6 digits or biometric
     equivalent
   - Automatic device lock after no more than 2 minutes of
     inactivity
   - Operating system updates installed within 14 days of
     vendor release
   - Use of the Company-approved authenticator application
     for any service requiring multi-factor authentication
   - Disabling of cloud backup services that may capture
     Company data, including consumer photo and document
     backup services for any folders containing work content

Public app store applications used for Company business must
be from the Company-approved catalog. Side-loading of
applications is prohibited.

================================================================
6. SOFTWARE INSTALLATION RULES
================================================================

Employees may not install unapproved software on Company
devices, consistent with the IT Security Policy (IT-POL-0203,
Section 5). Approved software is listed in the Company
Software Catalog and is automatically available through the
self-service portal on Company laptops. Requests for new
software, including browser extensions and command-line
utilities, must be submitted through the Software Request
Portal for security and licensing review.

Software identified as unapproved on a Company device will be
removed automatically by endpoint management tooling without
prior notice. Repeated installation of unapproved software,
or attempts to bypass software controls, may result in
disciplinary action.

================================================================
7. DEVICE LOSS REPORTING
================================================================

Lost, stolen, or otherwise compromised devices must be
reported immediately and no later than 1 hour from
discovery, consistent with the Incident Reporting SOP
(SOC-SOP-0418, Section 7) and the IT Security Policy
(IT-POL-0203, Section 10). Reports must be submitted to the
Security Operations Center via the 24x7 hotline or portal,
and should include:

   - Device type, asset tag if known, and last known
     location
   - Time and circumstances of loss or theft
   - Any data the device may have held in local storage
   - Whether a police report has been filed (for theft)

The Security Operations Center will initiate remote wipe,
credential rotation, and access revocation procedures.
Employees must not delay reporting in an attempt to recover
the device themselves; recovery efforts may be coordinated
with IT after the report is filed.

================================================================
8. DATA STORAGE RESTRICTIONS
================================================================

The following data storage restrictions apply to all devices
regardless of ownership:

   - Customer data must not be stored on personal devices
     under any circumstances, consistent with the Data
     Privacy Policy (DPO-POL-0309, Section 4)
   - Confidential and Restricted data, as defined in
     DPO-POL-0309 Section 3, must not be stored on removable
     media unless the media is Company-issued, encrypted, and
     tracked
   - Local storage of Company data on Company devices is
     permitted only where the Company-managed encryption and
     backup configuration is active
   - Personal cloud storage services (consumer Dropbox,
     personal Google Drive, iCloud Drive for work content,
     and similar) must not be used for any Company data
   - Screenshots, photographs, or screen recordings of
     Confidential or Restricted data are prohibited except
     where explicitly authorized in writing

================================================================
9. REMOTE WORK DEVICE EXPECTATIONS
================================================================

Employees working remotely or on a hybrid basis under the
Remote Work Policy (HR-POL-0061) must additionally:

   - Use Company devices exclusively for work involving
     Confidential or Restricted data
   - Position the device so that the screen is not visible to
     household members or visitors during work involving
     Company data
   - Connect to Company systems only through the Company VPN
     or approved zero-trust access broker
   - Avoid leaving Company devices unattended in vehicles,
     public spaces, or unsecured locations
   - Ensure that any peripheral connected to a Company device
     was purchased from an approved vendor or has received
     individual IT approval, as required by IT-POL-0203
     Section 6 and FIN-POL-0117 Section 6

Home office equipment purchases are governed by FIN-POL-0117
Section 5 and require manager approval before purchase.

================================================================
10. SECURITY REQUIREMENTS
================================================================

All devices accessing Company systems must meet the baseline
security controls defined in the IT Security Policy
(IT-POL-0203, Section 7), including:

   - Full-disk encryption
   - Active Company endpoint detection and response (EDR)
     agent (Company devices) or Company MDM enrollment
     (approved personal devices)
   - Screen lock with a maximum 5-minute inactivity timeout
     on laptops and 2-minute timeout on mobile devices
   - Operating system and application patching within the
     timelines defined in IT-POL-0203
   - Restricted local administrator privileges on Company
     devices

Tampering with security tooling, including disabling the EDR
agent, removing the MDM profile, or attempting to bypass
disk encryption, is treated as a security incident and
reported under SOC-SOP-0418.

================================================================
11. EXCEPTIONS
================================================================

Exceptions to this policy may be granted only where a
documented business need exists and compensating controls
are in place. All exceptions must be requested through the
Security Exception Management process and approved by the
CISO or a delegated authority. Exceptions are time-bound,
do not exceed 12 months without re-review, and do not
establish precedent.
